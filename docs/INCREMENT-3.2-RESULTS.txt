Increment 3.2 — Accounting Voucher Management

Continued from 63a558e on feature/increment-3.2. The checkpoint's voucher module was retained and completed through targeted fixes and verification. Increment 4 is outside this change.

Accounting behavior

- Journal Vouchers (JV) use balanced journal lines without payment or check metadata.
- Check Vouchers (CV) require a payee, designated active same-client cash/bank asset account, positive exact amount, check number, and valid check date. The designated account's credits must equal the payment.
- Cash Receipts (CR) require a payer/source, designated account, and positive exact amount matching that account's debits. Check metadata is rejected.
- Cash Disbursements (CD) require a payee, designated account, and positive exact amount matching that account's credits. Check metadata is rejected.

Each voucher owns exactly one authoritative General Journal entry. No separate financial postings or balances were introduced. Draft/Needs Correction records can be edited by authorized preparers; submission, independent review, and authorized Owner/Office Manager posting reuse the existing journal workflow. Correction clears approval; posting revalidates metadata, open period, active accounts, balancing, evidence integrity, and the review digest. Posted journals contribute once to the General Ledger, Trial Balance, Income Statement, Balance Sheet, and Accounts Ledger.

Client assignment and accounting permissions apply to list, create, edit, show, evidence, and print actions. Supporting attachment versions remain private and permission checked. Issued voucher identity and reference are fixed. Per-client/per-type sequence allocation, UUID request replay protection, and duplicate checks use the existing client transaction lock and database constraints. Reviewed/posted metadata and posted financial lines retain their model/database protections.

Remaining limitations

- Numbering remains the checkpoint's disclosed provisional TYPE-000001 sequence, separate for each client and type; no annual reset or approved statutory series policy is inferred.
- Cash/bank designation is explicit selection of an active Asset account. There is no new cash/bank account subtype or automatic counterpart inference.
- Supporting documents remain optional under the existing workflow; attached versions must pass integrity and permission checks.
- Vouchers record accounting evidence, not actual payment execution, bank/check clearing, or reconciliation. No void/cancel/reversal workflow is introduced. Issued references are retained; posted corrections require separately authorized journal entries.
- Printing uses browser print layouts. Printer hardware and statutory form certification are outside this verification.
- Existing financial reports retain their posted-ledger basis and provisional unclosed-earnings presentation documented in Increment 3.1. No closing entries or year-end transfers are generated.

No real accounting data, production migrations, .env changes, or Increment 4 work are part of this completion.
