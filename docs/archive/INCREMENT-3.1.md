The four financial reports use the existing Posted-only General Ledger. The user confirmed that Chapter 3 adds no further layouts or year-end conventions and approved separate unclosed-earnings presentation as a provisional development convention, not RBCIA accounting policy. No voucher management or automatic closing has been implemented.

3. **Calculation methodology.** The shared ledger supplies exact integer-cent balances and date ordering. No new financial storage, SQL aggregation, floating-point totals, or dashboard-derived financial figures were introduced. Every aggregate, sign conversion, and reconciliation difference uses checked integer arithmetic; overflow stops generation.

   Trial Balance uses each account's cumulative closing balance through the reporting date. Positive signed balances enter the debit column; negative balances enter the credit column. Opening is included exactly once. Income Statement uses only movements within the inclusive selected date range: revenue equals negative signed revenue movement, expenses equal signed expense movement, and net income equals revenue less expenses. Debit/credit columns show each account's net movement, not gross journal turnover. Unusual balances remain signed in the statement column.

   Balance Sheet uses all posted history through the reporting date. Assets retain debit-positive signs; liabilities and posted equity are credit-positive. The configured fiscal year containing that date is required. Prior-year unclosed earnings equal negative revenue/expense balances before that fiscal year's start; the current-year component equals negative revenue/expense movements from fiscal-year start through the reporting date. Their sum is the actual unclosed revenue/expense balance. They are shown separately from posted equity accounts, including any explicitly posted retained earnings. No transfer or balancing amount is generated. Both the underlying debit/credit equality and `Assets − (Liabilities + Posted equity + Prior unclosed earnings + Current-year component)` are checked. A mismatch is prominently marked UNRECONCILED on screen and print.

   Accounts Ledger reuses the existing service and its deterministic date/journal-ID/line-ID ordering, opening carry, running balances, and closing totals. The financial report includes every matching movement in one request; Stage 2.5's paginated General Ledger and selected-page printing remain available.

9. **Accounting and operational limitations.** This is a Posted-ledger reporting basis, not a certified set of statutory financial statements. Arithmetic reconciliation does not prove completeness, correct account classification, or completion of all postings. Period status does not imply that financial closing journals exist. Manually posted closing or correction journals contribute like every other posted transaction; no journal-type metadata currently supports a separate pre-closing Income Statement. In particular, a prior-year closing journal posted in a later fiscal year offsets that later year's revenue/expense movement component; that component must not be assumed to represent operating performance without reviewing such journals. The reports disclose this ledger basis and the provisional treatment. RBCIA must confirm closing conventions before a formal retained-earnings/closing workflow is introduced.

   Statements group by the five authoritative account classifications; no invented current/non-current, tax, cash-flow, or equity subcategories are inferred from account names. Custom Income Statement ranges may span fiscal years; their exact dates are displayed. Selecting a configured period overrides both custom dates. Missing fiscal-year configuration prevents Balance Sheet generation rather than assuming a calendar year. Reports read fresh posted data per request, so new/backdated posts may change a later print request. Ledger calculation scans relevant history, and full Accounts Ledger output retains all selected movements; high-volume benchmarking, streaming exports and frozen report snapshots are future work. Browser printing is provided without new PDF dependencies.
